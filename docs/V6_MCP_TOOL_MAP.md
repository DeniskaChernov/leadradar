# Lead Radar V6 — Internal MCP Tool Map

## Read-Only Tools (Automatic Execution)

### 1. `lead.*`
- `lead.search`: Search leads by role, status, score, or keyword.
- `lead.explain_score`: Detailed factor breakdown and evidence citations for a lead.

### 2. `audience.*`
- `audience.dna`: Extract Audience DNA summary for a group of contacts or segment.

### 3. `competitor.*`
- `competitor.opportunities`: Discover commercial opportunities and unanswered demand for a competitor.

### 4. `rattan.*`
- `rattan.company_analysis`: Classify artificial rattan market player role (wholesaler, reseller, manufacturer).

### 5. `google.*`
- `google.openings`: Fetch B2B venue opening queue.

---

## Write & Budget Tools (Human-in-the-Loop Approval Required)

### 1. `crm.*`
- `crm.assign_lead`: Assign manager to a lead (requires human approval).

### 2. `meta.*`
- `meta.create_campaign_draft`: Create PAUSED Meta Ads campaign draft (requires human approval).
